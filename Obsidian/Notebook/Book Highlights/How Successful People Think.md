## How to become a better thinker
1. Expose yourself to good input: read, listen, observe
2. Expose yourself to good thinkers: seek those who actively think and look for new challenges
3. Choose to think good thoughts: making thinking a deliberate action
4. Act on your good thoughts: "think things through—then follow through" - Eddie Rickenbacker
5. Allow your emotions to create another good thought: act to feel, dont wait to feel to act
6. Repeat the process: one good idea doesnt make a good life
