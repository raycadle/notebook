## Cash Flow Types
### Poor Cash Flow
income = salary -> pays expenses
assets = none
liabilities = many -> increases expenses
income = limited to no growth

### Rich Cash Flow
income = salary + dividends -> buys assets
assets = many -> pays expenses
liabilities = few -> limited to essentials
income = infinite growth

## Number 1 rule
Know the difference between assets and liabilities, and **buy assets!**

Financial IQ entails learning about:
1. accounting - read and understand money and financial statements
2. investing - science of money making money
3. markets - supply and demand
4. the law - utilizing a corporation to aid exponential growth and protect against excessive tax and litigation

5 reasons financially literate people might not acquire assets:
1. fear - overcoming the fear of losing money
2. cynicism - overcoming cynicism
3. laziness - busy people are often most lazy
4. habits - life reflects habits more than education does
5. arrogance - ego + ignorance
