## Metadata
- Author: [[George C. Clason]]
- Full Title: The Richest Man in Babylon
- Category: #books
- Document Tags: [[shortlist]] 
- Summary: "The Richest Man In Babylon" teaches that true wealth comes from building a steady income, not just having money. It emphasizes the importance of wise investments and learning the trade before making financial decisions. The story illustrates that there is enough wealth for everyone if they seek it wisely.
