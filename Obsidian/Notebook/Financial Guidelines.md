# Financial Guidelines

Money plays a huge role in attaining freedom in today's society. Below are a few steps to take in order to attain freedom.

1. Understand your finances
    - Get oriented with the 6 most important numbers:
      - Income
      - Expense
      - Cashflow
      - Assets
      - Liabilities
      - Net Worth
2. Restructure your finances
    - Set goals for the 6 numbers:
      - Income: 6 figures per year passively
      - Expense: minimal, allowing modest comfort
      - Cashflow: positive 5 figures per year
      - Assets: numerous cashflow generating
      - Liabilities: covered by assets' cashflow
      - Net Worth: evergreen (self-sufficient)
3. Create additional sources of income
    - Look at available asset classes:
      - Real estate (rentals, leases)
      - Stocks (shares, bonds, and ETFs)
      - Currency (Forex)
      - Commodities (gold, silver, precious metals)
      - Business (products and services)
4. Invest in your team
    - Build a team that can support you
    - Give lavish praise to your team
5. Just GO!
    - With the foundation finished, start building your empire
    - Reinvest surplus income back into assets
