## Purpose
A non-profit organization dedicated to empowering learners of all generations through apprenticeships with masters in their respective fields.

## Guidelines
Hiii-Minds follows guidelines founded on strong ethics, mutual respect, and societal benefit.

### Pillars
1. Transparency
2. Education
3. Passion

### Transparency
All organizational processes are available for scrutiny, enabling superb transparency with our members and the community.

### Education
Educating is not done in a classroom. There are no textbooks, uniforms, nor tuition.

Instead, we organize apprenticeships with our mentor partners to give our learners hands-on experience in the industry of their choosing.

We also host monthly seminars, lead by our mentors, to facilitate knowledge sharing between mentors and the community.

Education includes, but is not limited to:
- Finance & Investing
- Software & Hardware Development
- Design & Fabrication
- Engineering and Mechanics

### Contributions
We regularly host expos to show the community the progress of our apprentices and to facilitate growth within our organization and the community.

Annual donations are distributed to organizations which have shown their dedication to empowering the community for the better.

## Apprenticeship Program

Each applicant must fill out the application form and meet certain criteria to be accepted into the program.

Upon acceptance, the applicant will be awarded the "Potential" title, where they become eligible to choose and apply for apprenticeship with a "Master" from whichever industry they choose (e.g. Engineering, Management, etc.)

If a Master acknowledges the Potential's application, they put them through 90 days of probation, whereby the Potential may or may not be paid, as determined by the Master.

After 90 days has passed, both the Master and Potential are interviewed to determine their compatibility with each other. The Potential's compatibility with the industry will also be evaluated.

If the Master decides they would like to take on the Potential, the Potential must decide whether they would like to become an Apprentice for that Master.

Upon becoming an Apprentice, they must complete a 2-year period with their Master, during which the Master must compensate the Apprentice equivalent to an entry-level employee.

The Master can decide at any time to renounce the Apprentice, at which time the Apprentice will be returned to the title of Potential. The Apprentice may also choose to renounce the Master, also returning to the Potential title.

An appeal into any renouncing can be made by both the Apprentice and the Master. At that time, both parties must submit substancial reason why they wish to renounce the other or vise versa. The verdict is decided by the board of directors.

After the 2-years apprenticeship, the Apprentice is promoted to full-time staff, if the Master deems them ready. If not, the Apprentice may choose to stay another year as Apprentice, or look for a new Master.
