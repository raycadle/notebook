# The First Law
Gold comes freely, and abundantly, to any master who sets aside a tenth of their earnings to secure an estate for their future and the future of their family.

# The Second Law
Gold works diligently for any master who provides profitable work, thus quickly multiplying to work even harder.

# The Third Law
Gold clings to the protection of a cautious master who invests it only after seeking the advice of those wise in its investment.

# The Fourth Law
Gold quickly deserts any master who invests it into any business or purpose with which they are not familiar, or which is not approved by those wise in its investment.

# The Fifth Law
Gold flees any master who would force it to impossible earnings by trusting their inexperience and romantic desires, or who listen to the alluring advice of tricksters and schemers.
